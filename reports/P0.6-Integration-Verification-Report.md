## 1. Executive Summary

## 7. Integration Limitations & Production Safeguards

1. **Network Connectivity:**
   Both NVIDIA and Bodhan operate via cloud endpoints. While responses are fast (< 1s for OCR, ~3s for LLM reasoning), network outages require local retry backoffs, which are handled via `tenacity` in `src/ai/llm_service.py` and `src/ai/ocr_service.py`.
2. **Rate Limits & API Costs:**
   The hybrid document pipeline enforces native text bypass as the primary defense against rate limit exhaustion and unnecessary cloud spend. Scanned documents are rendered at 300 DPI to strike an optimal balance between OCR accuracy and Base64 payload size.
3. **Reasoning Token Management:**
   Nemotron 3.5 utilizes reasoning tokens (`chat_template_kwargs.enable_thinking=True`). The service architecture cleanly isolates reasoning content from answer content, ensuring token quotas are tracked without leaking internal thoughts into student assessment prompts.
